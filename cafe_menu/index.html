Our Story

Nestled in the heart of Mountain View, Mountain Brew Cafe opened its doors in 2020 with a simple mission: to provide the perfect cup of coffee in a warm, welcoming environment. We source our beans directly from small-batch suppliers and roast them in-house to ensure the freshest, most flavorful cup possible.

Our pastries and treats are made fresh daily using local ingredients whenever possible. Come experience the Mountain Brew difference!
